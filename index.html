How to Play

Read the story, pick choices, and watch your stats and inventory evolve. Your decisions steer the narrative and unlock unique endings.

  • Health: drops on dangerous paths; if it hits 0, your journey ends.
  • Reputation: rises with kindness, falls with cruelty; affects choices.
  • Inventory: key items enable special options and better endings.

You can edit the story data file story.json to change or add nodes.
